# Manufacturer Documents

These documents from the manufacturer contain the technical background of the Trilobit.

- {download}`Trilobit specifications (PDF) <../assets/docs/Trilobit_specs.pdf>`: weight, size, depth rating, recording, battery and clock specifications as well as the sensor specifications. See also {ref}`01_introduction`.
- {download}`Trilobit geophone configurations (PDF) <../assets/docs/Trilobit_galperin_configuration.pdf>`: how the three geophones are arranged in the Galperin configuration, their polarity, and the matrix that converts g1, g2 and g3 into x, y and z. See also {ref}`08_xyz_conversion`.
