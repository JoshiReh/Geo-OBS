# X, Y, Z Conversion

The three geophones of the Trilobit are mounted in a Galperin configuration: each sensor is tilted by 54.7° from the vertical. The recorded channels g1, g2 and g3 therefore have to be converted into the conventional x, y and z components. This notebook does that conversion.

The background, including the exact orientation and polarity of the sensors in the Trilobit and the conversion matrix, is described in the manufacturer's note {download}`Trilobit geophone configurations (PDF) <../assets/docs/Trilobit_galperin_configuration.pdf>`.
